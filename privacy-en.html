Privacy Policy for YNPixel

Last updated: March 26, 2026

At YNStudios, we respect your privacy. This policy describes how YNPixel handles data in our mobile application.

1. Information Collection

YNPixel is primarily a local creation tool. We do not require account registration. However, to improve user experience, we collect non-identifiable technical data and usage events through third-party services.

2. Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Firebase Analytics to collect information on how the application is used (e.g., which tools are used most, save frequency, or technical errors). This data helps us optimize the app and is not associated with your personal identity.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of YNPixel uses Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (Android Advertising ID) to show ads and analyze performance. In applicable regions, we request your prior consent for personalized or non-personalized ads through an official Google consent form.

4. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Premium version payments are handled exclusively by Google Play. YNStudios does not have access to your financial data or credit cards.

5. Storage Permissions

The app requests storage access only to save, export, or import your pixel art files (.ynpixel, PNG, GIF). We do not access your private photos or personal files unrelated to the app's functionality.

6. Data Deletion

Since we don't store user personal identity data on our servers, "data deletion" is achieved by uninstalling the app from your device.

7. Contact
